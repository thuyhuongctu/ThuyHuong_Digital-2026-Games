## Full description (tối đa 4000 ký tự)

```
⚓ Every game is a page of Vietnamese history.

Sử Việt Games (Việt History Games) is a portal of beautifully crafted
history games set in authentically Vietnamese worlds — from the royal
trading port of Vân Đồn in 1149 to the pirate seas of the Gulf of Tonkin
in 1780. Play offline, with no ads and no data collection.

🏮 VÂN ĐỒN — THE FIRST TRADING PORT (1149)
Build the kingdom's first international trading port, founded by royal
edict of King Lý Anh Tông. Welcome merchant ships from Song China, Japan,
Java and Siam; open pottery kilns, silk workshops and pearl rafts; weather
storms and repel raiders. Rise from a fishing hamlet to the First Trading
Port of Đại Việt — then test yourself in a challenge mode recreating the
historic Battle of Vân Đồn (1288).

🏴‍☠️ BẮC HẢI ĐẢO — PIRATES OF THE GULF OF TONKIN (1780)
Sail a bat-wing junk among the limestone karsts of Hạ Long Bay. Trade fish
sauce, silk, agarwood and pearls across six historic ports; hunt pirate
ships, survive storms, meet the Sacred Dragon, and defeat the pirate lord
Hắc Giao to be crowned King of the Northern Isles. Four playable captains,
each with a distinct perk.

🌊 BẮC HẢI ĐẢO 3D
The same pirate world rebuilt in full 3D: a chase camera behind your junk,
rolling waves, towering karsts, storm weather and the golden Dragon winding
through the sky.

✨ FEATURES
• 3 complete games on one historical timeline
• Play offline — no internet needed after install
• No ads, no accounts, no data collection
• Generative background music on the Vietnamese pentatonic scale
• Full touch controls, saves stored on your device
• Real events, ports, trade goods and figures from Vietnamese history

📚 Made for players, students and anyone curious about the history of
Vietnam. New games will be added throughout 2026.
```
